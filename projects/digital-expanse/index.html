Digital Expanse

A home page that can stay unfinished

A personal-site experiment built around prose, numbered plates, and a margin for what is current.

The question

Can a personal site grow without needing to be finished first?

Inside the project

The context

The site moved through ledger, chart, and manuscript approaches. Its current source treats the page as a manuscript with plates and keeps earlier explorations available.

Recorded choices

  • Use familiar serif prose and condensed annotations rather than a new typographic identity for every iteration.
  • Keep content separate from the HTML template and use plain browser-readable output.
  • Preserve older directions as part of the working record.

A story about the website as an ongoing collection, where unfinished work has a place and previous decisions remain legible.
