Tide Now

One day of useful information

A mobile-first tide checker for fishing: one day, a scrubbable tide curve, high and low times, and nearby weather.

The question

What is enough information for a quick decision by the water?

Inside the project

The context

The project is intentionally narrow. It shows a selected day rather than a dashboard, combining tide predictions with weather as supporting context.

Recorded choices

  • Keep the primary view centered on one selected day.
  • Make the tide curve, next high and low, and movement cues easy to inspect together.
  • Treat weather as optional context rather than a dependency for showing tide information.

Working notes

A story about useful constraints: a small decision, a focused view, and a secondary data source that is allowed to fail.

A proposed case-study direction, not a claim about a shipped outcome.
